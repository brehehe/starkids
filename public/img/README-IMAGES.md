## Recommended Image Specifications

### Hero Images
- **Size**: 1920x1080px
- **Format**: JPG
- **Quality**: High (80-90%)
- **Content**: Professional medical facility photos

### Branch Images
- **Size**: 800x600px
- **Format**: JPG
- **Quality**: High (80-90%)
- **Content**: Exterior/interior clinic photos

### Doctor Photos
- **Size**: 400x400px
- **Format**: JPG
- **Quality**: High (80-90%)
- **Content**: Professional headshots with medical attire

## Creating Placeholder Images

Jika tidak memiliki gambar asli, Anda dapat menggunakan:

1. **Unsplash.com** - Free medical/healthcare photos
2. **Pexels.com** - Free stock photos
3. **Generated avatars** untuk doctor photos
4. **AI-generated images** menggunakan tools seperti DALL-E

## Example URLs untuk placeholder:

```html
<!-- Hero Images -->
<img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1538108149393-fbbd81895907?w=1920&h=1080&fit=crop" alt="Clinic Hero">

<!-- Branch Images -->
<img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1519494026892-80bbd2d6fd0d?w=800&h=600&fit=crop" alt="Clinic Branch">

<!-- Doctor Photos -->
<img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1612349317150-e413f6a5b16d?w=400&h=400&fit=crop" alt="Doctor">
```
